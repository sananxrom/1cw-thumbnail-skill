Do not attach every image to every generation. The working default is current Eddie source + current guest + XR + robotic-arm, optionally a background reference. Add a recent render only for a specific layout/finish need. Number references to match actual attachment order. Original style images contain other people and headlines; explicitly prohibit copying them into the new episode.

Archive prompts are in [prompt-index.md](prompt-index.md). Latest paired PNGs are bundled; older prompt-only examples are still useful but their historical outputs and guest inputs are not all included. Read [lessons.md](lessons.md) for superseded decisions.

The [manifest](asset-manifest.json) records hashes and dimensions of bundled images and hashes of prompts. Original machine paths are historical provenance, not dependencies. No font binaries, credentials, PSDs or private episode transcripts are bundled.

The old series thumbnails and approved examples retain Eddie’s previous hair/clothes as historical artwork. Use them for design only; the new portrait overrides their identity details. Historical prompt documents remain unchanged as records and must be adapted to the current portrait.
