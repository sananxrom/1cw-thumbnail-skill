Do not attach every image to every generation. The working default is original Eddie + current guest + XR + robotic-arm, optionally a background reference. Add a recent render only for a specific layout/finish need. Number references to match actual attachment order. Original style images contain other people and headlines; explicitly prohibit copying them into the new episode.

Archive prompts are in [prompt-index.md](prompt-index.md). Latest paired PNGs are bundled; older prompt-only examples are still useful but their historical outputs and guest inputs are not all included. Read [lessons.md](lessons.md) for superseded decisions.

The [manifest](asset-manifest.json) records hashes and dimensions of bundled images and hashes of prompts. Original machine paths are historical provenance, not dependencies. No font binaries, credentials, PSDs or private episode transcripts are bundled.
