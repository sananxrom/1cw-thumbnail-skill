# Prompt construction template

Replace bracketed episode fields; remove unused lines before calling the tool. This is scaffolding, not extra copy for the image. Exact recent examples: [Adrian](prompts/adrian-ai-biotech-godmode-v1-prompt.txt) and [Anirban](prompts/anirban-1024-qubits-v1-prompt.txt).

```text
Create ONE finished premium photographic 1CW/XROM YouTube thumbnail, landscape 16:9 PNG, a fresh render using original identity references.

INPUT REFERENCES IN ACTUAL ATTACHED ORDER:
1. Original Eddie portrait: strict identity anchor for LEFT host.
2. [Guest name] source photograph: strict RIGHT guest identity, expression and clothing.
3. Original XR thumbnail: typography/composition/style ONLY.
4. Original robotic-arm thumbnail: style and topic-integrated host gesture ONLY.
[5. Optional episode background or additional style reference: specify exact role.]
Do not reproduce guests, words or unrelated props from style references.

EXACT APPROVED COPY AND LINE BREAKS:
[line 1]
[line 2]
[optional line 3 or 4]
No additional text.

TYPE: [explicit line-by-line font, relative size and colour hierarchy]. Large type Morganite ExtraBold, exceptionally tall narrow heavy letterforms. Small supporting type Avenir Next Bold. Morganite tracking +10 Photoshop units, 0.01em. Centre group on whole canvas, approximately x=50%; balance row widths while allowing chosen emphasis. Generous vertical spread and comfortable gaps, roughly 20–26px at 1280x720 scale when appropriate. Upward-to-right shear ~6.5°, vertical strokes upright. White and yellow-to-golden-orange emphasis. Soft restrained down-right shadow; faded near-black diagonal panel. Type can overlap torsos/props or extend beyond the panel; keep faces and all copy readable. Do not force a long phrase into an overwide row.

EDDIE LEFT: faithfully preserve original face, facial proportions, eyes/nose/mouth, age, complexion, moustache, small grey goatee and swept-up wavy salt-and-pepper hair. Familiar dark textured waistcoat and white short-sleeved shirt unless approved otherwise. [Approved gesture, expression, accessory and gaze.] Keep expression close enough to source for unmistakable likeness. Anatomically natural hands. Large edge-cropped figure with torso, face unobscured.

GUEST RIGHT: [Name and source-specific identity details]. Preserve face, hair, expression and outfit. [Explicitly approved exception, if any.] Crop or naturally extend torso/clothing to balance Eddie's head size and body framing. Minimal enhancement; do not redesign the person.

BACKGROUND: [Approved simple contextual environment and one main motif]. Broad forms and room depth, darkened/softened with inward edge vignette, few micro-details. [Topic palette.] Keep background subordinate to people/text. No clutter of miniature objects or unnecessary floating interfaces.

FINISH: natural photographic faces, smooth skin lighting transitions, subtle pores and clean hair edges; no blotchy, swirled, waxy, etched or over-sharpened skin. Restrained scene lighting on subjects. [Optional sparse faint golden dust mainly lower foreground/clothing.] No unrequested badges, logos, names or episode numbers. One final PNG with exact copy and faithful identities.
```

Do not copy past guest permissions into a new episode. The old Annie prompt forbids overlap with the petri dish; that is superseded by the user's later general permission to overlap props. Read current design rules before reusing an archived prompt.
