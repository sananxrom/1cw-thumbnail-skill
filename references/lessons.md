# Lessons from the originating series

## What became the stable workflow

The user supplies title, guest picture and episode context, sometimes exact text and a backdrop. First propose concise copy and visual direction; generate only after confirmation. One-shot built-in renders are preferred. PSD production is a fallback if the result requires editing. A new episode starts with original photographic identities, not the latest generated faces.

Original XR and robot-arm designs are the strongest style anchors because topic, Eddie pose and accessories work together. All six original designs belong to the same style; their palettes and line counts differ. The latest two renders were followed by the user's positive feedback that the images were now working well, and are current calibration examples.

## Refinements in order of relevance

- After Vineeth: Eddie must look like Eddie; preserve original likeness ahead of pose drama. Hair is especially characteristic.
- After Le Cong: darken the background with edge vignette; reduce small-object clutter. Apply to future work, not unsolicited revisions of approved images.
- After Aubrey and Vijay: crop OR extend the guest naturally to fit the composition and torso proportions. Do not rigidly preserve the original crop.
- After Annie: centre the text group on the whole canvas; it can overlap or pass behind props, such as her petri dish. Optional offset depends on the words, not a mandatory style trait.
- After Babak: avoid one overlong “BRAIN-INSPIRED CHIPS” row. Break long text into compact rows.
- Typography: Morganite ExtraBold for large text, Avenir Next Bold for smaller text; centre aligned, widths balanced by sizing. +10 tracking and slightly generous vertical gaps. Text may extend outside the faded panel.
- Shadows: less harsh and mildly directional. Golden dust should be faint and unify foreground, not compete.

## Failure modes

Early Partha brain-mapping revisions accumulated blotchy/painted skin texture. Later face restoration variants helped, but a fresh rebuild damaged the approved text layout. Therefore return to original portrait references, consolidate the brief, explicitly preserve approved copy/layout and avoid long edit chains. Do not use the v6 rebuild as the preferred typography reference.

Generative font matching is visual rather than deterministic. Distinguish requested font from verified actual typesetting. Requested aspect ratio also differs slightly from actual generated file dimensions; inspect before stating exact size.

## Original PSD observations

The source PSDs were inspected read-only in the originating work. Reported canvas 1280 × 720; Morganite ExtraBold and Avenir Next Bold text; transforms corresponding to about 6.5° shear; separate text, image, generative/lighting and particle layers. Optional particle layers used lighten blending at roughly 35–42% opacity. These findings informed visual prompts; they are not exact settings of the generated PNGs. PSDs may contain hidden/older copy different from the final PNGs. Prefer final PNGs as appearance references. PSDs are not included in this portable core.
